## 1. Overview & Accreditation Purpose
As part of your graduation clearance at CTU-Naga Extension Campus and for state university leveling (SUC Leveling) and accreditation audits (**Q-Pro / PESO**), every graduating senior is required to create an account in **CTU-Naga Alumni Connect** and complete the **Institutional Graduate Tracer Survey**.

> **Data Privacy Notice (Republic Act 10173)**: All personal information and salary responses are treated with strict confidentiality. Salary data is aggregated into anonymous brackets for institutional quality assurance and will never be shown to peers.

---

## 2. Step 1: Account Registration via Student ID
To ensure that only legitimate CTU-Naga graduates register, the platform uses an **Eligibility Registry**:

1. Navigate to the Alumni Connect registration page: `http://localhost:5173/register` (or the campus deployment URL).
2. Enter your official **CTU Student ID Number** (e.g., `2022-12345` or `1234567`).
3. Enter your **Birth Date** to verify your identity against campus registrar records.
4. Once verified, provide:
   - Your active **Personal Email Address** (avoid using school emails that may expire upon graduation).
   - A secure password (minimum 8 characters with numbers and symbols).
5. Verify the one-time passcode (OTP) sent to your registered email address.

---

## 3. Step 2: Completing the Mandatory Graduate Tracer Survey (Clearance Requirement)
Upon your first login, a notification banner will prompt you to complete the **Graduate Tracer Survey**:

1. Click on **Surveys** in the main navigation or click **Answer Clearance Survey**.
2. Complete all required survey sections:
   - **Degree Program & Graduation Batch**: Confirm your program (BSIT, BIT, BSEd, BTLEd, BEEd).
   - **Curriculum Relevance**: Rate how relevant your coursework and laboratory training were to industry expectations.
   - **Competencies Developed**: Select competencies gained during your stay at CTU (technical skills, communication, problem-solving).
   - **Initial Employment Status**: Indicate whether you are already employed, self-employed, or seeking opportunities.
3. Click **Submit Survey Responses**.
4. Your account profile will automatically receive a **Clearance Completed Badge**, notifying the campus registrar and alumni coordinator that your clearance tracer requirement has been fulfilled.

---

## 4. Step 3: Updating Initial Career & Employment Status
Keep your career information up-to-date so CTU-Naga and industry partners can connect with you:

1. Click on your profile picture in the top-right corner and select **My Profile**.
2. Click **Edit Alumni Profile** and navigate to the **Career Details** section.
3. Select your current status:
   - **Employed**: Fill in your Job Title, Company Name, Industry Sector, and Employment Type (Full-time, Part-time, Contract).
   - **Self-employed / Freelance**: Enter your trade, freelance role, or business name.
   - **Seeking Opportunities**: Helps alumni coordinators refer you to campus job fairs and hiring employers.

---

## 5. Step 5: Uploading Proof of Employment (Accreditation Dossier / Q-Pro)
For university accreditation audits, university evaluators require verified evidence of alumni employment:

1. In your **Profile** under **Current Employment**, click **+ Upload Proof** or open the edit modal.
2. Select your **Proof Document Type**:
   - **Certificate of Employment (COE)**
   - **Official Company ID**
   - **Employment Contract / Formal Appointment Letter**
   - **Business Permit / DTI Registration** *(for self-employed graduates)*
3. Paste the cloud document link (e.g., Google Drive link, OneDrive link, or image URL) to your proof.
4. Submit the update.
5. The status will display as **Pending Admin Verification**. The CTU-Naga alumni administrator will review the document and mark it **Verified (Q-Pro)**.

---

## 6. Step 5: Exploring Job Opportunities & Direct Employer Applications
The platform is designed to fast-track your employment without administrative delays:

1. Navigate to **Job Opportunities** from the top menu.
2. Filter openings by **Industry Sector**, **Job Type**, or **Alumni Exclusive** postings.
3. **Number of Vacancies**: Postings clearly indicate mass-hiring openings (e.g., *20 Openings*).
4. **Hiring Posters / Flyers**: View official company flyers by clicking **View Hiring Poster / Flyer**.
5. **Direct Application**:
   - Click **Apply on Company Site** to submit your credentials directly into the employer's HR system.
   - For positions with listed recruiter contacts, use the direct **HR Email** or **Phone Hotline** provided in the job description to follow up on your application immediately.

## 7. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q: Why do I need to complete the survey before graduation clearance?
**A:** State universities are evaluated based on the employability and curriculum feedback of their graduates (Q-Pro & SUC Leveling). Your response ensures the campus maintains high educational standards and accreditation.

### Q: Will other students see my salary or my Certificate of Employment?
**A:** **No.** Under the Data Privacy Act (RA 10173), your salary and proof documents are strictly private. Only designated administrative auditors can view verification documents for accreditation purposes.

### Q: What if I am still job hunting at the time of graduation?
**A:** Select **Seeking Opportunities**. This allows the CTU Naga Placement Office and Alumni Association to notify you directly of upcoming job fairs, recruitment partnerships, and vacancy alerts.
